    Διδακτικό και ερευνητικό σεμινάριο με γενικό θέμα:

    Συναρτησιακή Ανάλυση και Άλγεβρες Τελεστών

    Πρόγραμμα: Κάθε Τρίτη, 15:00-17:00 στην Αίθουσα Γ31

    7η συνάντηση: Τρίτη 26 Μαΐου 2026

    Oμιλητής: Ευγένιος Κακαριάδης (ΕΚΠΑ)

    Τίτλος: Product Systems and their Operator Algebras VIII

    Περίληψη: Product systems provide a common language and context to encode<br>
     geometric structures such as semigroups, graphs, dynamics etc. Their<br>
     operator algebras have been under thorough study in the past 40 years with<br>
     much success giving rise to two objects: (a) the maximal Fock-covariant<br>
     C\*-algebra, and (b) the minimal strong covariant C\*-algebra. There are<br>
     further links between these objects by using the nonselfadjoint operator<br>
     algebra generated in the Fock representation. In this talk we will give a<br>
     characterisation of injective Fock covariant representations of a product<br>
     system and we will give a positive resolution to the reduced Hao-Ng<br>
     isomorphism problem for generalised gauge actions by discrete groups.
