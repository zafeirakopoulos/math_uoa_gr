    Συναρτησιακή Ανάλυση και Άλγεβρες Τελεστών

    Πρόγραμμα: Κάθε Τρίτη, 15:00-17:00 στην Αίθουσα Γ31<br>
     6η συνάντηση: Τρίτη 12 Μαΐου 2026

    Oμιλητής: Απόλλωνας Παρασκευάς (ΕΚΠΑ)

    Τίτλος: Product Systems and their Operator Algebras VI

    Περίληψη: Product systems provide a common language and context to encode<br>
     geometric structures such as semigroups, graphs, dynamics etc. Their<br>
     operator algebras have been under thorough study in the past 40 years with<br>
     much success giving rise to two objects: (a) the maximal Fock-covariant<br>
     C\*-algebra, and (b) the minimal strong covariant C\*-algebra.<br>
     There are further links between these objects by using the nonselfadjoint<br>
     operator algebra generated in the Fock representation. In this talk we will<br>
     give a characterisation of injective Fock covariant representations of a<br>
     product system and we will give a positive resolution to the reduced<br>
     Hao-Ng isomorphism problem for generalised gauge actions by discrete groups.
