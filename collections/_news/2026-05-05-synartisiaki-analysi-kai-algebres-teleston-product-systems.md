    Τίτλος: Product Systems and their Operator Algebras VI

    Περίληψη: Product systems provide a common language and context to encode geometric structures such as semigroups, graphs, dynamics etc. Their operator algebras have been under thorough study in the past 40 years with much success giving rise to two objects: (a) the maximal Fock-covariant C\*-algebra, and (b) the minimal strong covariant C\*-algebra. There are further links between these objects by using the nonselfadjoint operator algebra generated in the Fock representation. In these talks I will give an overview of the latest results in the field.
